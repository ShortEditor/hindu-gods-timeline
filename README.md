# Hindu Gods Timeline - Research (Open Edition)

A fact-check of a widely circulated reconstruction of the Hindu gods' timeline: the Puranic yuga system, the claim-by-claim evidence, and where the places of the epics (Lanka and the Rama Setu / Adam's Bridge, Ayodhya, Hastinapur, Kurukshetra, Dwarka, Sinauli, Saraswati, Kailash) sit on today's map and in the archaeological record.

- Every claim is checked against live sources, with proof screenshots and quotations (sources numbered and linked).
- Includes a full license and attribution table, so the document can be republished as open source.
